# Pattern 18 Quick Reference Card

## When Your Child Is Difficult

**"That was unkind."**
"That was unkind, and I don't allow people to treat me that way — including you. I'm going to walk away now, and I'll be here when you're ready to try again with kindness."

**When they're dismissive:**
"You can disagree — I still meant every word."

**When they call you "dramatic":**
"Calling someone dramatic is a tactic people use to silence truth. I know that trick. It doesn't work here."

**When they defend the other parent:**
"You're allowed to love them. That doesn't mean I have to pretend I was treated well."

**When they mock your calm approach:**
"I don't perform for people. I speak the truth once, and I walk away when I'm not being respected."

**When they question your sources:**
"Does it matter where the words came from if they're true?"

**When they're condescending:**
"Okay. I said what I needed to say. Whether or not it sinks in is up to you."

---

## Core Truths (For You)

**When triggered:**
"My trigger is not the problem. The abuse was."

**When doubting yourself:**
"I am not dramatic — I am healing. I do not owe anyone silence."

**When exhausted:**
"My boundaries are not betrayal. I'm breaking the cycle."

**When hopeless:**
"I am the safe parent. I am the steady ground. Even if they can't see it now, one day they may."

---

## Before Hard Conversations

1. Take 3 slow breaths
2. Feel your feet on the ground
3. "I do not have to convince anyone. I just have to stay rooted in truth."
4. You are ready

---

## Repair Script (After Being Triggered)

**Full version:**
"Hey. Last night, I got triggered. I've been through a lot, and sometimes I don't handle it perfectly. I'm sorry if it felt heavy. How we treat people matters — especially the people closest to us, when no one's watching. That's where real character lives. I love you, and I'm here."

**Short version:**
"I know things got tense — I just want you to know I love you, I'm still learning too, and I'm here."

**Lightest version:**
"I'm still your parent whether you like it or not. I love you. I screw up sometimes. You do too. We'll figure it out."

---

## Responding to the Co-Parent

**Formula:** Brief. Factual. Boundary-holding.

✓ "I've confirmed this is during my parenting time. No changes are needed."
✓ "I'm focused on what's best for [child]."
✓ "I won't be engaging with [that topic]."
✓ "No response needed."

✗ Don't JADE (Justify, Argue, Defend, Explain)
✗ Don't engage with bait
✗ Don't match their tone

---

## Grounding (5-4-3-2-1)

When spiraling:
- 5 things you can SEE
- 4 things you can TOUCH
- 3 things you can HEAR
- 2 things you can SMELL
- 1 thing you can TASTE

---

## After Time with the Other Parent

**Day 1:** "Hey. Welcome back." (Neutral. Warm. No expectations.)

**Give 24-48 hours** before deep talks. Let their nervous system settle.

**If they're cold:** "It's hard when you come back and I feel like the enemy. I love you — and I'm still me."

---

## Remember

You are doing sacred work. You're trying to break a generational cycle while standing in the middle of it.

Your steadiness is the counterweight to the chaos.

Even when they can't see it, you are the safe parent.

The story isn't finished.
